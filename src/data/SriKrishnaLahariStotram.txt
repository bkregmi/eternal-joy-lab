Here is the complete layout of the 8 verses of the Sri Krishna Lahari Stotram, structured with the original Sanskrit text, the corresponding IAST (transliteration), and the complete Meaning all grouped together for each verse. [1] 
------------------------------
## 🕉️ श्री कृष्ण लहरी स्तोत्रम् (Sri Krishna Lahari Stotram)## Verse 1

* Sanskrit:

कदा वृन्दारण्ये विपुलयमुनातीरपुलिने
चरन्तं गोविन्दं हलधरसुदामादिसहितम् ।
अहो कृष्ण स्वामिन् मधुरमुरलीमोहन विभो
प्रसीदेति क्रोशन्निमिषमिव नेष्यामि दिवसान् ॥ १ ॥

* IAST:

kadā vṛndāraṇye vipula-yamunā-tīra-puline
carantaṁ govindaṁ haladhara-sudāmādi-sahitam |
aho kṛṣṇa svāmin madhura-muralī-mohana vibho
prasīdeti krośan-nimiṣam-iva neṣyāmi divasān || 1 ||

* Meaning:
When will that day come when I wander through the forests of Vrindavan upon the vast, sandy banks of the pure Yamuna River? There, I long to see Lord Govinda strolling alongside His brother Haladhara (Balarama) and His dear friend Sudama. Crying out intensely, "O Krishna! O my Master! O All-pervading One who captivates everyone with the sweet music of Your flute, please be merciful to me!", I wish to pass my days so absorbed in devotion that years pass by in the blink of an eye.

------------------------------
## Verse 2

* Sanskrit:

कदा कालिन्दीयैर्हरिचरणमुद्राङ्किततटैः
स्मरन् गोपीनाथं कमलनयनं सस्मितमुखम् ।
अहो पूर्णानन्दाम्बुजवदन भक्ताैकललन
प्रसीदेति क्रोशन्निमिषमिव नेष्यामि दिवसान् ॥ २ ॥

* IAST:

kadā kālindīyair-hari-caraṇa-mudrāṅkita-taṭaiḥ
smaran gopīnāthaṁ kamala-nayanaṁ sasmita-mukham |
aho pūrṇānandāmbuja-vadana bhaktaikalalana
prasīdeti krośan-nimiṣam-iva neṣyāmi divasān || 2 ||

* Meaning:
When will I walk along the shores of the Yamuna River (Kalindi), which are completely adorned and sanctified by the footprints of Sri Hari? While walking, I want to constantly remember Gopinatha (the Lord of the Gopis), visualizing His lotus-like eyes and gently smiling face. Crying out, "O Embodiment of absolute bliss, whose face is like a blooming lotus, O protector and lover of Your devotees, be pleased with me!", I wish to spend my days in this divine ecstasy.

------------------------------
## Verse 3

* Sanskrit:

कदाचित्खेलन्तं व्रजपरिसरे गोपतनुजैः
कुतश्चित्सम्प्राप्तं किमपि लसितं गोपललनम् ।
अये राधे किं वा हरसि रसिके कञ्चुकयुगं
प्रसीदेति क्रोशन्निमिषमिव नेष्यामि दिवसान् ॥ ३ ॥

* IAST:

kadācit-khelantaṁ vraja-parisare gopa-tanujaiḥ
kutaścit-samprāptaṁ kimapi lasitaṁ gopa-lalanam |
aye rādhe kiṁ vā harasi rasike kañcuka-yugaṁ
prasīdeti krośan-nimiṣam-iva neṣyāmi divasān || 3 ||

* Meaning:
Sometimes, I long to see Him playing joyfully in the neighborhoods of Vraj with the cowherd boys—that radiant, extraordinary young boy of the cowherds who appeared from some mysterious divine realm. I long to witness His sweet, loving pastimes as He playfully teases, "O Radha! O beautiful queen of divine rasas, why have You hidden My garment?" Witnessing these playful pastimes and crying out, "O Lord, bless me!", I wish to spend all my days.

------------------------------
## Verse 4

* Sanskrit:

कदाचिद्गोपीनां हसितचकितस्निग्धनयनं
स्थितं गोपीवृन्दे नटमिव नटन्तं सुललितम् ।
सुराधीशैः सर्वैः स्तुतपदमिदं श्रीहरिमिति
प्रसीदेति क्रोशन्निमिषमिव नेष्यामि दिवसान् ॥ ४ ॥

* IAST:

kadācid-gopīnāṁ hasita-cakita-snigdha-nayanaṁ
sthitaṁ gopī-vṛnde naṭam-iva naṭantaṁ sulalitam |
surādhīśaiḥ sarvaiḥ stuta-padam-idaṁ śrī-harim-iti
prasīdeti krośan-nimiṣam-iva neṣyāmi divasān || 4 ||

* Meaning:
Sometimes, I wish to see Him standing in the midst of the Gopis, dancing with exquisite grace like a master actor, while the Gopis gaze at Him with surprised, laughing, and deeply affectionate eyes. This is the very same Sri Hari whose lotus feet are continuously praised by all the rulers of the heavens (Indra and the Gods). Calling out to that supreme dancer, "Be pleased with me!", I want my days to pass like a fleeting moment.

------------------------------
## Verse 5

* Sanskrit:

कदाचित्सच्छायाश्रितमभिहान्तं यदुपतिं
समाधिस्वच्छायाञ्चल इव विलोलैककरम् ।
अये भक्तोदाराम्बुजवदन नन्दस्य तनय
प्रसीदेति क्रोशन्निमिषमिव नेष्यामि दिवसान् ॥ ५ ॥

* IAST:

kadācit-sacchāyā-śritam-abhihāntaṁ yadu-patiṁ
samādhi-svacchāyāñcala iva vilolaika-karam |
aye bhaktodārāmbuja-vadana nandasya tanaya
prasīdeti krośan-nimiṣam-iva neṣyāmi divasān || 5 ||

* Meaning:
Sometimes, I long to see the Lord of the Yadus (Krishna) resting under the cool, soothing shade of a tree, waving one of His beautiful hands like the moving border of a garment, inviting devotees into peaceful meditation. Calling out, "O generous Lord of the devotees, with a face like a lotus, O beloved son of Nanda Baba, show me Your grace!", I wish to spend my days fully immersed in Your thought.

------------------------------
## Verse 6

* Sanskrit:

कदाचित्कालिन्यास्तततरुकदम्बे स्थितममुं
स्मयन्तं साकूतं हृतवसनगोपीसुतपदम् ।
अहो शक्रानन्दाम्बुजवदन गोवर्धनधर
प्रसीदेति क्रोशन्निमिषमिव नेष्यामि दिवसान् ॥ ६ ॥

* IAST:

kadācit-kālinyāstata-taru-kadambe sthitam-amuṁ
smayantaṁ sākūtaṁ hṛtavasana-gopī-suta-padam |
aho śakrānandāmbuja-vadana govardhana-dhara
prasīdeti krośan-nimiṣam-iva neṣyāmi divasān || 6 ||

* Meaning:
Sometimes, I wish to visualize Him perched high up on the branches of the Kadamba tree on the banks of the Yamuna River, smiling knowingly and playfully having taken away the clothes of the meditating Gopis to teach them absolute surrender. Praying to Him, "O Lord who brings joy to Indra, O Lifter of the Govardhan Hill, be pleased with me!", I wish to spend my life crying out for Your vision.

------------------------------
## Verse 7

* Sanskrit:

कदाचित्कान्तारे विजयसखमिष्टं नृपसुतं
वदन्तं पार्थेति नृपसुत सखे बन्धुरिति च ।
भ्रमन्तं विश्रान्तं श्रितमुरलिमास्यं हरिममी
प्रसीदेति क्रोशन्निमिषमिव नेष्यामि दिवसान् ॥ ७ ॥

* IAST:

kadācit-kāntāre vijaya-sakhamiṣṭaṁ nṛpa-sutaṁ
vadantaṁ pārtheti nṛpa-suta sakhe bandhuriti ca |
bhramantaṁ viśrāntaṁ śritamuralim-āsyaṁ harimamī
prasīdeti krośan-nimiṣam-iva neṣyāmi divasān || 7 ||

* Meaning:
Sometimes, I want to see Him in the forests acting as the dearest friend and guide to Prince Arjuna (Vijaya), affectionately calling out to him, "O Partha! O prince, my friend, my brother!" I long to see Sri Hari wandering, resting, and holding the divine flute close to His lips. Calling out to this compassionate friend of Arjuna, "Please be merciful to me!", I desire to pass my days.

------------------------------
## Verse 8

* Sanskrit:

कदा द्रक्ष्ये पूर्णं पुरुषममलं पङ्कजदृशं
अहो विष्णो योगिन् रसिकमुरलीमोहन विभो ।
दयां कर्तुं दीने परमकरुणाब्धे समुचितं
प्रसीदेति क्रोशन्निमिषमिव नेष्यामि दिवसान् ॥ ८ ॥

* IAST:

kadā drakṣye pūrṇaṁ puruṣam-amalaṁ paṅkaja-dṛśaṁ
aho viṣṇo yogin rasika-muralī-mohana vibho |
dayā kartuṁ dīne parama-karuṇābdhe samucitaṁ
prasīdeti krośan-nimiṣam-iva neṣyāmi divasān || 8 ||

* Meaning:
When, at last, will I truly behold this flawless, Supreme Divine Being with lotus-like eyes? O Lord Vishnu! O supreme Master of Yoga! O enchanting player of the flute who relishes the spiritual essence of love! It is only fitting for You, who are an ocean of boundless mercy, to shower grace upon a poor, helpless soul like me. Constantly weeping and crying out, "O Lord, have mercy and show Yourself!", I wish to spend every single day of my life.

------------------------------

इति श्रीवासुदेवानन्दसरस्वती कृत श्री कृष्ण लहरी स्तोत्रम् ॥
iti śrī-vāsudevānandasarasvatī kṛta śrī kṛṣṇa laharī stotram ||
